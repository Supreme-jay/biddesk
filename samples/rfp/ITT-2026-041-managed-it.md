# INVITATION TO TENDER ITT-2026-041

# Provision of Managed IT Services to Brynmawr Borough Council

# Section 3 — Service Requirements

3.1.1 The Supplier shall operate a service desk available 24 hours per day, 365 days per year, including all public holidays.

3.1.2 The Supplier shall acknowledge all Priority 1 incidents within 15 minutes of logging and shall provide resolution within 4 hours.

3.1.3 The Supplier must maintain a minimum core infrastructure availability of 99.9% measured monthly, excluding agreed planned maintenance.

3.1.4 The Supplier shall provide a named Service Delivery Manager who will chair monthly operational service reviews with the Authority.

3.1.5 Service management processes shall be aligned to ITIL 4 or a demonstrably equivalent framework.

3.1.6 The Supplier shall submit monthly performance reports within 5 working days of month end, including service level achievement and trend analysis.

3.1.7 Where service levels are not met, the Supplier shall apply service credits automatically without requiring the Authority to submit a claim.

3.1.8 The Supplier shall provide a self-service portal accessible to all Authority end users for logging and tracking incidents.

# Section 4 — Information Security and Data Protection

4.1.1 The Supplier shall hold and maintain certification to ISO/IEC 27001 throughout the contract term.

4.1.2 The Supplier must hold current Cyber Essentials Plus certification and shall provide evidence of annual recertification.

4.1.3 All Authority data shall be encrypted in transit and at rest to a minimum of AES-256 or equivalent recognised standard.

4.1.4 Multi-factor authentication shall be enforced for all privileged and remote access to Authority systems.

4.1.5 The Supplier shall deploy critical security patches within 14 days of vendor release and emergency patches within 48 hours.

4.1.6 The Supplier must notify the Authority of any confirmed personal data breach within 24 hours of confirmation.

4.1.7 All Authority data shall be stored and processed exclusively within the United Kingdom. No data shall be transferred outside the UK without prior written consent.

4.1.8 The Supplier shall undertake annual penetration testing by a CREST or equivalent accredited provider and share the results with the Authority.

4.1.9 The Supplier shall support the Authority in responding to requests made under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 within 5 working days of referral.

4.1.10 The Supplier must maintain documented business continuity arrangements with a Recovery Time Objective not exceeding 4 hours.

# Section 5 — Accessibility and Welsh Language

5.1.1 All user-facing digital services provided under this contract shall conform to WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a minimum.

5.1.2 The Supplier shall provide service desk support in Welsh and English, treating both languages equally in accordance with the Welsh Language Standards.

5.1.3 All written communications, portal interfaces and documentation issued to end users shall be available bilingually in Welsh and English.

5.1.4 The Supplier shall report annually on compliance with the Authority's Welsh Language Scheme.

# Section 6 — Social Value and Sustainability

6.1.1 The Supplier shall submit a Carbon Reduction Plan confirming commitment to achieving Net Zero by 2050, in accordance with PPN 06/21.

6.1.2 The Supplier shall report annually on Scope 1, Scope 2 and relevant Scope 3 greenhouse gas emissions attributable to this contract.

6.1.3 The Supplier shall commit to creating a minimum of two apprenticeship positions within the Authority's administrative boundary during the contract term.

6.1.4 The Supplier must demonstrate that no less than 30% of contract spend is directed to local small and medium sized enterprises.

6.1.5 The Supplier shall be an accredited Living Wage Employer or shall commit to accreditation within 12 months of contract award.

6.1.6 The Supplier shall publish an annual Modern Slavery Statement in accordance with Section 54 of the Modern Slavery Act 2015.

6.1.7 The Supplier shall pay all subcontractors within 30 days and report quarterly on payment performance.

6.1.8 The Supplier shall provide an Equality, Diversity and Inclusion policy and report annually on workforce diversity relating to this contract.

# Section 7 — Transition, Personnel and Exit

7.1.1 The Supplier shall deliver full service transition within 12 weeks of contract award at no additional cost to the Authority.

7.1.2 The Supplier shall manage the transfer of affected personnel in accordance with TUPE regulations and shall bear all associated costs.

7.1.3 All personnel with access to Authority systems shall hold a minimum of BPSS clearance prior to commencing work.

7.1.4 The Supplier shall maintain and update an Exit Plan, submitted within 90 days of contract commencement.

7.1.5 On termination the Supplier shall provide up to 60 days of transition assistance and return all Authority data in an agreed open format.

# Section 8 — Commercial and Financial

8.1.1 The Supplier shall hold professional indemnity insurance of not less than 5 million pounds for the duration of the contract.

8.1.2 The Supplier must provide three years of audited accounts demonstrating financial standing appropriate to the contract value.

8.1.3 The Supplier shall provide three referenceable contracts of comparable scope and value delivered within the last five years.

8.1.4 Should the Authority require additional sites to be brought into scope, the Supplier shall apply the agreed rate card without renegotiation.

# Service Desk Operation

Our service desk operates from Manchester and Glasgow with 24 hours a day, 7 days
a week, 365 days a year coverage. All analysts are UK-based and security cleared
to BPSS as standard, with SC clearance available on request. The desk is
accredited to the Service Desk Institute three-star standard.

Contact channels include telephone, email, a self-service web portal and a
Microsoft Teams integration. Average speed of answer over the last 12 months was
28 seconds with an abandonment rate below 3 percent.

# Incident Priority and Response Targets

Incidents are classified P1 through P4 on agreed impact and urgency criteria.

Priority 1, total service loss affecting multiple users: response within 15
minutes, resolution target 4 hours, 99 percent achievement.

Priority 2, significant degradation or single critical user affected: response
within 30 minutes, resolution target 8 hours.

Priority 3, standard incident with workaround available: response within 2 hours,
resolution target 2 business days.

Priority 4, service request or minor issue: response within 4 hours, resolution
target 5 business days.

# Service Level Agreement and Credits

Service levels are measured monthly and reported through a customer-accessible
dashboard with raw data available on request. Where a service level is missed we
apply service credits calculated as a percentage of the monthly charge, rising
from 2 percent for a single miss to 10 percent for sustained failure across three
consecutive months. Service credits are applied automatically without the
customer needing to claim.

# Availability Commitment

We commit to 99.9 percent availability for core managed infrastructure measured
monthly excluding agreed planned maintenance windows. Planned maintenance is
notified 10 working days in advance and scheduled outside core hours.

# Reporting and Service Review

Monthly service reports cover volumes, service level achievement, trend analysis
and continual improvement actions. A named service delivery manager chairs a
monthly operational review and a quarterly strategic business review with the
customer's senior stakeholders.

# ITIL Alignment

Service management processes are aligned to ITIL 4 covering incident, problem,
change, configuration, release and service request management. Our change
advisory board meets weekly with emergency change authority available continuously.

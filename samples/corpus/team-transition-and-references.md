# Team Structure and Resourcing

Northgate employs 148 staff across three UK offices. The delivery team proposed
for public sector contracts comprises a named account director, a service
delivery manager, a technical lead and a pool of second and third line engineers.
Staff turnover over the past three years averaged 11 percent, below the sector
average of 18 percent.

# Certifications and Skills

Our engineering team holds current certifications including Microsoft Certified
Azure Solutions Architect Expert, Cisco CCNP Enterprise, VMware VCP-DCV, and
CompTIA Security+. We fund continuing professional development with a minimum of
five training days per engineer per year and maintain a skills matrix reviewed
quarterly against the contract's technical requirements.

# TUPE and Staff Transfer

We have completed 14 TUPE transfers over the past six years, covering between 3
and 61 transferring staff. Our approach includes early consultation, a dedicated
HR transition lead, guaranteed protection of terms and conditions, and a 90-day
post-transfer engagement programme. We have never had a TUPE transfer result in
an employment tribunal claim.

# Transition and Mobilisation

Standard mobilisation runs to a 12-week plan with defined gates: discovery and
due diligence in weeks 1 to 3, knowledge transfer and documentation in weeks 4 to
7, shadow running in weeks 8 to 10, and service commencement with hypercare in
weeks 11 and 12. Hypercare provides double-staffed support for the first 30 days
at no additional charge.

Transition is delivered at no cost to the customer on contracts above three years
in duration. A named transition manager reports weekly against the plan with a
documented exit from each gate requiring customer sign-off.

# Exit Management

We provide a documented exit plan within 90 days of contract commencement, kept
current throughout the term. On exit we commit to full knowledge transfer,
handover of all documentation and configuration, and up to 60 days of
post-termination assistance. All customer data is returned in an agreed open
format with certified deletion thereafter.

# References and Comparable Contracts

Northgate currently delivers managed IT services to three local authorities, two
NHS trusts and a higher education institution. Our largest public sector contract
covers 2,400 end users across 31 sites with a annual contract value of 1.4 million
pounds, running since 2021 and extended twice. Written references are available
on request and site visits can be arranged.

# Financial Standing

Northgate has traded profitably for 11 consecutive years with turnover of 18.2
million pounds in the last financial year. Audited accounts are filed at Companies
House. We carry professional indemnity insurance of 5 million pounds, public
liability of 10 million pounds and employer's liability of 10 million pounds.

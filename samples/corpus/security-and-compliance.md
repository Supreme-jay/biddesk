# Information Security Management

Northgate Technology Services operates an Information Security Management System
certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, first certified in 2019 and recertified in 2025.
Our certificate number is IS-694412 and the scope covers managed infrastructure,
service desk operations and hosted application support across all UK sites.

# Encryption and Data Protection

All customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256.
Encryption keys are managed through a hardware security module with quarterly key
rotation. We maintain a documented data retention schedule and support secure
deletion with certificate of destruction on contract exit.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration
Z8841207, and act as data processor under UK GDPR. Our Data Protection Officer
is a named individual with direct reporting to the board.

# Access Control and Authentication

Access follows least-privilege principles enforced through role-based access
control. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all administrative access
and all remote connections. Privileged access is brokered through a session
recording gateway and reviewed monthly. Joiner, mover and leaver processes are
automated with same-day revocation on termination.

# Vulnerability and Patch Management

We operate a documented patch management policy with critical security patches
deployed within 14 days and emergency patches within 48 hours of vendor release.
Continuous vulnerability scanning runs against all managed estates with monthly
authenticated scans and annual CREST-accredited penetration testing.

# Incident Response

Our security incident response plan is tested twice yearly through tabletop
exercise. Incidents are triaged within 30 minutes of detection. We commit to
notifying the customer of any confirmed personal data breach within 24 hours,
supporting the customer's own 72-hour regulatory notification obligation.

# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

We maintain ISO 22301 aligned business continuity plans covering our own
operations, tested annually with documented results available to customers.
Standard recovery objectives are RTO of 4 hours and RPO of 1 hour, with backup
data replicated across two geographically separate UK data centres.

# Innovation Fund 2026 — Application Form

## About this fund

The Innovation Fund supports small businesses developing technology solutions
for the public sector. Awards of £25,000–£100,000 are available for projects
of 6–12 months duration.

## Application questions

1.1) Describe your organisation and its relevant experience in delivering
technology projects. (max 300 words, worth 10 marks)

1.2) What problem does your project address? Explain the need and who
benefits from a solution. (max 500 words, worth 15 marks)

2.1) Describe your proposed solution in detail. How does it work, and what
makes it innovative compared to existing approaches? (max 750 words, worth 20 marks)

2.2) Provide a detailed project plan including key milestones, deliverables,
and timelines for the 12-month project period. (max 500 words, worth 15 marks)

3.1) How will you measure the success and impact of your project? Describe
your evaluation methodology and key performance indicators. (max 400 words, worth 10 marks)

3.2) Explain how the project outcomes will be sustained beyond the funding
period. What is your route to market or continued adoption? (max 400 words, worth 10 marks)

4.1) Provide a breakdown of the project budget, including staff costs,
equipment, subcontracting, and any other expenditure. (max 300 words)

4.2) Describe your team and their qualifications. How will you ensure the
project has the right skills and capacity? (max 400 words, worth 10 marks)

5.1) What risks could affect the successful delivery of your project?
Describe your risk mitigation strategy. (max 300 words, worth 10 marks)

5.2) How does your project align with the government's digital and data
strategy? Explain any relevant policy alignment. (max 300 words)

## Eligibility checklist

- Confirm your organisation is a UK-registered SME
- Confirm you have not received more than £500,000 in public grants in the last 3 years
- Describe any potential conflicts of interest
